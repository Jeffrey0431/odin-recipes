Mashed potatoes and meatballs

Homepage

Ingredients:

Meatballs

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 yellow onion, finely grated
  • 1 egg
  • 1 dl whipping cream
  • 0.5 dl breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tsp white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • Butter, for frying

Cream sauce

  • 2 tablespoons concentrated veal stock
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 dl whipping cream
  • 1 tablespoon Chinese soy sauce
  • Black pepper

Garnish

  • Lingonberries
  • Pickled cucumber

Instructions

  1. Peel and boil the potatoes in salted water until soft.
  2. Peel and grate the onion on the fine side of the grater into a bowl.
  3. Add the minced meat and stir until smooth.
  4. Roll the batter into nice meatballs, you will make about 25-30 pieces.
  5. Melt the milk, cream and butter in a saucepan on the side. Let it get hot.
  6. Drain the water from the potatoes and press them through a potato masher or sieve. Add the cream and stir until smooth. Season with salt, white pepper and black pepper.
  7. Heat a frying pan with butter. Fry the meatballs over medium heat until just cooked through. When the meatballs are done, remove and place on a plate.
  8. Add water and concentrated veal stock to the frying pan. Stir with a whisk at the bottom so all the good flavors come out. Let boil down to half.
  9. Pour in the whipped cream and soy sauce and reduce (boil together) to a nice sauce. Not too thick, not too thin. Season with freshly ground black pepper.
  10. Serve the meatballs with mashed potatoes, cream sauce, raw stir-fried lingonberries and pickled cucumber.
